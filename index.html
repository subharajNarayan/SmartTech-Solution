From Idea to Impact

Smart Tech Solution designs software solutions for the problem in front of you, not a product catalog. Since 2010 we have built web, mobile, and desktop systems for government agencies, NGOs, and private businesses, work that now runs in Nepal and in 10 countries across the Pacific and Africa. Whether you need custom enterprise software development for an existing team or a new platform built from a concept, our team of technology professionals, civil engineers, and geomatic engineers designs, builds, and supports it end to end. We stay past launch: every project moves through analysis, design and development, and ongoing support, so the software keeps working as your organization changes.
